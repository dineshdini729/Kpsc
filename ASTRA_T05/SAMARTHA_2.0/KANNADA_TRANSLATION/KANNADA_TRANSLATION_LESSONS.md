# Kannada translation: lessons carried forward (from the ES 2025-26 edition)

Written at the close of the Kannada Economic Survey edition (CH01–CH13 frozen, 07.10.2026). It records what that edition established, for use when the owner orders Kannada translation of SAMARTHA question papers. It does not change any governance. The detailed rules stay in ES_STYLE_SHEET_v1.1.md (and its v1.3 and erratum in the repository), ES_KANNADA_DIGEST.md, KANNADA_KNOWLEDGE_DIGEST.md and START_HERE_KANNADA.md.

## 1. Where the wording comes from (in this order)
1. **The official Kannada document for the subject.** Use its terms, spelling, spacing and phrasing, even where a "purer" or more common word exists. For the ES, this was the official Kannada Economic Survey. For papers, it is the official Kannada source for each subject: Survey, Budget, Acts, the Kannada Constitution text, and government orders.
2. **DSERT Kannada textbooks** for the general flow of the prose and for school-level science, history and geography terms.
3. **KPSC/KEA official Kannada papers** for how stems, statements and options are worded: the stem-pattern census from Phase 1.
4. The owner's polity glossary (Aadalitha Padakosha / DSERT based). Use it as a reference only, and check it against the sources above before relying on it.
5. Never invent an official term. If no official form exists, give the plain Kannada word with the English abbreviation in brackets at first use.

## 2. Language rules that held across 13 chapters
- Natural Kannada, never word-for-word. The text must read on its own, without the English beside it.
- English loanwords are fine where the Kannada word is rare. The official document's own usage wins.
- At first use, give the Kannada term with the abbreviation in brackets: «ಸುಸ್ಥಿರ ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ ಗುರಿಗಳು (SDG)», «ಆಶಾ ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತೆ (ASHA)». Do this at first use in each section, in the official's own form. Do not put a case ending before the bracket.
- Write ranks («2ನೇ») and counts of ten or more in digits. Write «ಶೇ.» or «%» as the official does, and «ರೂ.» rather than ₹.
- Do not use «-ದ್ದರ» forms. Do not use figurative «ಓದು», «ಓಡು» or «ಹೊರು». Do not carry English metaphors over.
- "Against" or "compared with" is «…ಗೆ ಹೋಲಿಸಿದರೆ», not «ಎದುರು».
- Call each figure what the official calls it (share, allocation, expenditure, proposal), and compare like with like.
- Use «ಮಾತ್ರ» only where the English says "only" or the official prints it.
- No comma before «ಮತ್ತು».
- Countries and currencies in full: «ಅಮೆರಿಕನ್ ಡಾಲರ್». Expand an institution at first use: «ಭಾರತೀಯ ಬಾಹ್ಯಾಕಾಶ ಸಂಶೋಧನಾ ಸಂಸ್ಥೆ (ISRO)».
- Where the official prints a name in two forms (for example the goal tiles and the goal list), use the fuller statement consistently. Record the choice once.

## 3. Facts inside the translation
- Correct a wrong figure or fact from the official Kannada document first, then the official English. Every correction gets a register row with a class:
  - OFFICIAL-TEXT
  - OFFICIAL-OPERANDS
  - OFFICIAL-TABLE-RANK
  - EVIDENT-SLIP
  - UNSUPPORTED (dropped)
  - VERBATIM-UNRESOLVED
- If a count contradicts a list or table in the same text, it is an EVIDENT-SLIP, unless the official itself prints that count.
- If an official figure contradicts the official's own other figures, keep it as printed and conclude nothing.
- If a claim is neither stated nor contradicted by the official, keep it as written.
- Do not escalate discrepancies to the owner one by one. Settle them from the sources and record each one.

## 4. Extra rules for question papers (not needed in the ES)
- The Kannada must keep **exactly one correct option**. Check the key against the Kannada text, not only the English.
- Do not let the Kannada give the answer away:
  - case endings, gender or number agreement, or a repeated word in the stem must not point to one option;
  - option lengths stay balanced.
- Options are lettered a) b) c) d), never numbered. Statement numbering stays identical to the English.
- Use the KPSC Kannada forms for "how many" options and for assertion–reason, matching and "Mr X / Mr Y" formats, as the stem-pattern census records them. Never use forms invented for the occasion.
- Use the same Kannada term for a concept in a question and in its solution.
- Proper names (schemes, Acts, places, persons) follow the official Kannada spelling, so a student searching in Kannada finds them.

## 5. Process lessons
- **No session verifies its own work.** One session drafts and another verifies: the translation session drafts, and the builder checks, builds and audits facts. Gemini and ChatGPT are backup reviewers whose findings are dispositioned one by one.
- **Fast track:** open, draft, verify and build, corrections, rebuild, then freeze only after the owner writes "approved". Combine messages across items to save relays.
- **Never leave a session idle.** Plan the next task for each session before its current one ends.
- **Shared files:** wait up to 30 minutes for the other session's commit, then take the next free numbers. Stage only your own lines.
- **Read official pages as images** where the text layer drops characters or brackets (for example "(Proxy)").
- **Reviewers:**
  - ChatGPT sometimes only acknowledges its brief. Prompt it to proceed with the full audit in the block · check · problem · minimal fix format.
  - Check that a pasted Gemini reply is the reply, not the outgoing packet, and that it is complete.
- **Shipped means closed.** Once the owner ships a unit it is never reopened. Later improvements go only into units not yet shipped.
- **Owner's standing directions:**
  - Expedite.
  - Move ahead on recommendations without asking item by item.
  - Deliver editable Word files.
